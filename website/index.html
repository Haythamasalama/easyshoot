Privacy

Your screenshots never leave your Mac.

Screens show passwords, messages and client work. EasyShoot keeps all of it on your disk, in the folder you choose.

No account

Open it and start capturing. There is nothing to sign up for.

No cloud

Images are saved and copied locally. Nothing is uploaded, ever.

No tracking

No analytics and no telemetry. The app only goes online if you download a Google font.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is EasyShoot free?

Yes. It’s free and open source under the MIT license, with no trial and no subscription.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. On macOS 26 it uses the new Liquid Glass look.

macOS says it “could not verify” the app. What do I do?

Move EasyShoot to Applications, open it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You can also run xattr -cr /Applications/EasyShoot.app in Terminal.

Why does it need Screen Recording permission?

macOS requires it for any app that captures the screen. EasyShoot only uses it when you take a screenshot.

Where is the app after I open it?

EasyShoot lives in the menu bar at the top-right of your screen. Use the shortcuts, or open the app again to see the welcome window.
