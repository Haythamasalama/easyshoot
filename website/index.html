Everything inside

Four apps’ worth of work. One window.

Capture, mark up, frame and share without leaving EasyShoot. Every tool is a single key away.

Area, window, fullscreen

Drag over anything, or press Space to grab a whole window with its shadow.

Scrolling capture

Stitch long pages, chats and documents into one image. Sticky headers appear once.

Quick Access

A floating thumbnail after each capture. Copy, save, or drag it into any app.

Arrows and shapes

Arrows, lines, rectangles, ellipses, highlighter and pen. Every mark stays editable.

Numbered steps

Counters number themselves in order, so a walkthrough takes seconds.

Pixelate

Hide card numbers, emails and faces before you share.

Any font

Every font on your Mac, plus Google Fonts. Resize text by dragging its corner.

Arabic and right-to-left

Arabic and Hebrew write right-to-left with correct letter shaping.

Background tool

Gradients or solid colours, with padding, corners, shadow and aspect ratios.

Crop and resize

Crop freely or to 1:1, 4:3 and 16:9, and resize to an exact width.

Presets

Save a look and reuse it, or start each shot with your last settings.

Export

PNG, JPEG, HEIC or TIFF, with the final size shown before you save.

Your shortcuts

Record your own global shortcuts. In the editor, single letters switch tools.

Liquid Glass

Built for the newest macOS look, with a clean fallback on macOS 14 and 15.

Private by design

No account, no cloud, no analytics. Your screenshots stay on your Mac.

Install

Running in under a minute.

  1. 1Download EasyShoot.zip, unzip it and drag the app into Applications.
  2. 2Open it once. It isn’t notarized yet, so click Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security, or run the command.
  3. 3Allow screen recording in the Welcome window, then press ⌥⇧⌘4.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

# only if macOS blocks the first launch
$ xattr -cr /Applications/EasyShoot.app
$ open -a EasyShoot
