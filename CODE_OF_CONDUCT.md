# Code of Conduct

EasyShoot follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background, experience or language.
- Give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Harassment, insults, personal attacks and sharing someone's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@Haythamasalama](https://github.com/Haythamasalama), through the contact details on [haythamasalama.me](https://haythamasalama.me). Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions, or ban contributors, who don't follow this code.
